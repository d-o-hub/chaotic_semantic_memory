## 2. #706 evidence (why "no impact", not "small perf win")

Probe: standalone harness, counting `GlobalAlloc`, current `crates/csm-retrieval/src/hybrid.rs`
behaviour vs. the PR's variants. 1000 BM25 + 1000 HDC results, 80% disjoint ids,
`top_k = 10`, `rustc -O -C target-cpu=native`, 7 interleaved rounds × 4000 calls, medians.

| Path | Outputs | Allocs | Bytes | main ns/call | PR ns/call | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| map (both non-empty) | identical | 13 → 13 | 131,626 → 131,626 | 124,123 | 118,703 | −4.4% |
| single-list fast path | identical | 12 → 12 | 24,410 → 24,410 | 13,357 | 13,927 | **+4.3%** |
| empty | identical | — | — | — | — | — |

1. **The stated win is impossible.** `results.iter().map(..).collect::<Vec<_>>()` is
   `TrustedLen`-specialized: `Map<slice::Iter>` already reserves the exact length in one
   allocation. `Vec::with_capacity(len)` + `push` cannot "prevent unnecessary
   reallocations" — there are none to prevent.
2. **The guard removal is unreachable-false, not an optimization.** Above that point, the two
   fast-path early returns leave both slices non-empty; and even without them, folding min/max
   over an empty slice yields `range = -inf < f32::EPSILON`, taking the `weight` branch over an
   empty loop (no-op).
3. **No criterion attached** → AGENTS.md perf-evidence gate fails, the fourth instance of the same
   class (#529 → #605 → #689 → #706).
