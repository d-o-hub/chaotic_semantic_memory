## #950 — why "closed", in one paragraph each

**It is a re-filing of a measured-and-refuted claim.** #800 (2026-10-01/02) was roasted and
*measured* on this host: direct call `81.7 → 57.3 ns` (6/6 rounds), but the only product caller —
`crates/csm-core-lib/src/encoder.rs:251`, `base.permute(pos * position_stride)`, which reaches
`permute(0)` once per `encode` — moved `text_encoder/encode_short 2 684 → 2 734 ns` (3/6 rounds each
way), i.e. inside the run-to-run spread. #814 (2026-10-03) re-filed the same three lines and was
closed citing #800's numbers (`pull/814#issuecomment-5968629895`). #950 restates the claim again,
adding three `unsafe` blocks and carrying no evidence section. Rule 17: a resubmission must bring the
evidence the earlier roast demanded, not restate the claim.

**The claim's mechanism sentence is the same one #814 was refuted on.** "Saves 1 280 bytes of stack
writes per call" cannot hold while `permute` returns `Self` by value: the copy exists on every path,
`return *self` included. And the zero-fill being eliminated may not exist in the emitted code — the
recorded #783 codegen result is that rustc/LLVM already removes an overwritten `[0u128; 80]`
initializer. Not demonstrated ≠ no codegen delta; separating them needs disassembly or a bench.

**No existing benchmark can produce the cited number.** `benches/binary_benchmark.rs:29` benches
`BHVec10240::permute(321)` (a different type), and `321 % 128 = 65`, so neither new branch runs;
there is no `HVec10240::permute` bench at all. This is the #763/#909 "bench blindness" class.

**CI truth.** Run `40835994704` (head `78d4838`) had exactly one failing context, `commitlint`
(check-run `123320794383`), annotated by this repo's own ADR-0095 gate: *missing
`## Performance Evidence` section in the PR body*. `core` is in `scope-enum`, so the red is the
evidence gate, not formatting. Everything downstream was `skipping`.

**Correctness was never the objection.** The three `unsafe` sites are layout-sound. Worth recording
so nobody re-suggests the tidy alternative: `MaybeUninit::array_assume_init` is **not** available on
this repo's pinned toolchain — verified `E0658: use of unstable library feature
maybe_uninit_array_assume_init` against `rust-toolchain.toml`'s `1.88.0`, despite being stable upstream.

## Worth landing from #950, without the perf claim

Two assertions `main` does not have (`test_permute` pins only `v == v.permute(0)` and
`permute(128)`): `assert_eq!(v, v.permute(10240))` (the double-modulo identity) and a
`permute(1)` → `permute(10239)` round-trip (the non-word-aligned inverse). A `test(core)` PR with
those two lines and no source change merges on its own merits.
